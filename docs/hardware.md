# Hardware Architecture & Errata (Rev.1.0)

## Analog Front-End & Pinout
- **TIA:** AD8616 dual op-amp (`Rf = 5.1 MΩ`, `Cf = 10 pF`), photovoltaic mode.
- **Bias:** `VSHIFT ≈ 1.42 V` relative to GNDA for pseudo-differential bipolar swings on single 3.3V rail.
- **Sensors:** 2 × Hamamatsu S1087 photodiodes.
- **Power:** BQ24074 charger + TPS63031 buck-boost (shared digital/analog rail).

### Pin Mapping
| Peripheral | Signal | ESP32 GPIO | Direction |
|---|---|---:|---|
| ADS1220 | CS | GPIO5 | Output |
| ADS1220 | DRDY | GPIO16 | Input |
| ADS1220 | SCLK | GPIO18 | Output |
| ADS1220 | DOUT | GPIO19 | Input |
| ADS1220 | DIN | GPIO23 | Output |
| Control | LED Trigger | GPIO27 | Output |
| Debug | UART0 TX / RX | GPIO1 / GPIO3 | — |

## Known Issues & Requirements for Rev.1.1
- **Power Rail:** The analog front-end and ADC are powered directly from the shared 3.3 V rail generated by TPS63031, without a dedicated low-noise post-regulation stage. Switching ripple and digital supply noise may therefore couple into the precision analog path.  
  *Rev.1.1:* Add a dedicated low-noise analog supply using an LDO or appropriate post-regulation/filtering stage with sufficient voltage headroom.
- **Guard Ring:** Rev.1.0 guard structure was referenced to `GNDA` instead of the `VSHIFT` potential of the high-impedance TIA node.  
  *Rev.1.1:* Redesign the guard around the photodiode/TIA high-impedance nodes and reference it to `VSHIFT`, with parasitic capacitance considered during layout.
- **Debug:** No dedicated UART programming/debug header or JTAG access header was provided in Rev.1.0.
- **Metrology:** Long-term analog drift, SNR, and optical linearity pending validation on Rev.1.1.
